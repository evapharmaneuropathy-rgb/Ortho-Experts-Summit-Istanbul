TÜRKİYE

Istanbul, where two continents meet

The only city in the world spanning Europe and Asia, split by the Bosphorus Strait. Point Hotel Taksim sits on the European side, in the heart of the city's modern center.

WEATHER IN OCTOBER
☀
Mild & occasionally rainy

Typical daytime highs around 19°C (66°F), dropping to about 12°C (54°F) at night. Expect roughly 10 rainy days across the month — pack a light jacket and a compact umbrella, layers work best.

CUSTOMS & ETIQUETTE

Greetings: a handshake is standard; close friends may greet with a cheek kiss.

Dress: smart-casual is fine in the city; modest dress is expected when visiting mosques (shoulders and knees covered, headscarf for women).

Tipping: 5–10% at restaurants is customary; round up for taxis.

Friday prayer: some shops and restaurants near mosques pause briefly around midday on Fridays.
